01

Purpose and Scope

SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD provides refrigerators and related appliance products to customers, including students and households. This Store Policy sets out how SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD handles sales, product descriptions, grading, payments, delivery, customer care, warranty support, repairs, damage, returns, refunds, exchanges, complaints and service requests.

The policy is designed to make transactions clear before purchase, to record the condition of individual appliances, and to provide a consistent process when an issue is reported after delivery. It applies to all transactions unless a written transaction-specific term lawfully varies a provision of this policy.

SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD operates in accordance with applicable South African law, including the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 (CPA) and applicable privacy and electronic-communications requirements.

02

Product Categories and Condition Grading

SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD uses product grades to describe the apparent condition of the specific appliance being offered. Grading is a commercial condition description and must be read together with the product listing, photographs, disclosed issues and the applicable legal quality standards.

Grade What It Means What to Expect
New Condition Presented as new condition, little or no visible evidence of prior use beyond any specifically disclosed characteristic. Clean, presentable appearance and expected basic operating condition for the product category, subject to any written disclosure and applicable law.
A-Grade Pre-owned, reconditioned or previously used refrigerator selected because its overall cosmetic and operating condition is considered very good for its category. Minor signs of use may be present, such as light scratches, small marks or normal age-related wear. Any material known issue will be disclosed where relevant.
B-Grade Pre-owned, reconditioned or used refrigerator suitable for its intended purpose but with more visible signs of use than an A-Grade unit. More noticeable scratches, dents, fading, wear or other cosmetic imperfections may be present and reflected in the price. Material disclosed limitations will be stated where relevant.
Grade Principle A lower cosmetic grade does not give SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD permission to sell an appliance that is not reasonably suitable for its intended purpose or to remove a statutory consumer right.
03

Product Descriptions and Disclosed Characteristics

  • Product photographs are intended to represent the specific appliance where practical, but screens and lighting can affect the appearance of colours and surface marks.
  • Cosmetic wear that is visible or expressly disclosed is part of the described condition of a pre-owned or reconditioned appliance.
  • Where SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD knows of a material characteristic relevant to a customer decision, it may record that characteristic in the listing, quotation, invoice or written communication.
  • Product measurements, capacity descriptions, energy information and model specifications should be checked against the applicable manufacturer or product information where relevant.
  • Normal operating sounds may occur in refrigeration equipment. An unusual sound will be assessed on its actual circumstances and will not be dismissed solely because the appliance is pre-owned.
04

Individual Appliance Identification

Where reasonably practicable, SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD records the specific refrigerator supplied by make, model, serial number and/or internal stock reference. Photographs may be taken before dispatch and at delivery. This allows the condition of the actual appliance supplied to be distinguished from photographs of other units.

The transaction record may include the product grade, sale price, delivery details, pre-delivery test results, customer confirmations, service history and photographs. These records may be used for ordinary administration, after-sales support and lawful complaint handling.

05

Pre-Delivery Testing

Before delivery, SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD may conduct a reasonable basic operational inspection appropriate to the appliance. Testing may include:

  • Power-on operation
  • Basic cooling performance
  • Freezer operation where applicable
  • Compressor operation
  • Thermostat or temperature control
  • Door alignment and closing
  • Door seal condition
  • Visible leaks or obvious physical damage
  • Interior condition and accessories
  • Reasonable operating sounds

A pre-delivery test is evidence of the condition observed at the time of testing. It does not prevent a customer from raising a later genuine defect or exercising a statutory right.

06

Pricing, Quotations and Promotions

  • Prices are stated in South African Rand unless otherwise specified.
  • The applicable product price, delivery charge and separately priced service will be disclosed through the applicable listing, quotation, invoice or order confirmation.
  • Quotes may be subject to a stated validity period, stock availability and any conditions shown on the quote.
  • Promotional prices, bundle offers and discounts may have specific conditions, limited stock, closing dates or eligibility requirements, which will be communicated with the promotion.
  • A discretionary discount is a commercial concession and does not automatically change the underlying description or condition of the product.
  • Where a payment has been reversed, contested, charged back or cannot be verified, SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD may pause fulfilment or service while the payment status is investigated, subject to applicable law.
07

Payment Terms

  • Payment must be made using the payment method and timing stated on the quotation, invoice or order confirmation.
  • Proof of payment may be requested before an appliance is dispatched.
  • SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD does not automatically provide instalment plans, lay-bys or rental arrangements. Any such arrangement must be expressly agreed in writing before the transaction.
  • Where a deposit is accepted, the applicable deposit terms will be communicated to the customer in writing.
  • A customer should use the correct transaction reference where one is provided to reduce payment verification delays.
08

Order Confirmation and Stock Allocation

An enquiry or quotation is not necessarily a completed sale. Stock is allocated once SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD has confirmed the order and the required payment or other agreed condition has been satisfied.

Where a specific appliance has been reserved, the reservation period and any conditions attached to the reservation will be communicated in writing. SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD may release an unconfirmed reservation after the communicated reservation period expires.

09

Delivery Policy

  • Delivery charges, where applicable, are stated separately from the product price.
  • Delivery is made to the agreed location and is subject to reasonable access for the delivery team.
  • Campus and residence deliveries may be affected by security procedures, loading restrictions, access distances, stairs, elevators, parking arrangements, residence rules and waiting times.
  • The customer is responsible for providing accurate delivery information and for making the agreed delivery location reasonably accessible.
  • Additional delivery attempts, extended waiting, special handling, relocation, collection or other customer-requested services may attract separately disclosed charges where permitted by law.
  • Delivery timing may be affected by traffic, weather, security procedures, supplier delays and other circumstances reasonably outside the control of SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD.
10

Delivery Inspection and Acceptance

Customers should inspect the refrigerator as soon as reasonably practical after delivery. The customer should check the product identity, visible physical condition, accessories, door operation and basic operational concerns and should report any concern promptly.

SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD may request delivery confirmation by WhatsApp, photograph, electronic acknowledgement or signature. Such confirmation records receipt and the condition observed at that time; it is not intended to waive statutory rights concerning a latent or subsequently discovered defect.

11

Customer Responsibility for Use and Care

  • Use the refrigerator for its intended household purpose and in accordance with reasonable operating instructions.
  • Do not puncture, drill, cut, dismantle or interfere with refrigeration components.
  • Do not undertake technical repairs or modifications without appropriate authorisation where those actions may affect warranty or diagnosis.
  • Avoid overloading doors, shelves or fittings beyond their reasonable intended use.
  • Use an appropriate electrical supply and avoid unsafe electrical connections.
  • Keep the appliance reasonably accessible when inspection or service has been arranged.
  • Report serious operating concerns promptly so that the appliance can be assessed while relevant evidence is still available.
12

Warranty and Statutory Consumer Rights

SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD provides warranty support in accordance with applicable law and any additional express warranty specifically offered for the appliance. The warranty terms applicable to an individual transaction may also be stated on the invoice or product documentation.

Where the CPA or another applicable law provides a statutory remedy, SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD will administer the complaint and provide the remedy required by law. A product grade, prior use, customer acknowledgement or pre-delivery inspection does not by itself remove a statutory consumer right that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Where a repair is performed, any applicable legal rights relating to parts, labour or the repair itself will be observed.

13

How to Submit a Warranty or Product Complaint

A complaint should be submitted through the designated SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD communication channel and should include enough information for the appliance and reported issue to be identified:

  • Customer name and contact details
  • Invoice/order reference
  • Make and model
  • Serial number or stock reference where available
  • Description of the problem
  • When the problem was first noticed
  • Photographs or videos where reasonably possible
  • Information about any damage, modification, movement or third-party repair relevant to the issue

SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD may request further information and may require a reasonable opportunity to inspect or diagnose the refrigerator before determining the appropriate remedy, subject to applicable law.

14

Damage After Delivery

A refrigerator remains the customer's responsibility to handle with reasonable care after delivery. Physical damage can affect the appliance and can also affect the ability to diagnose later complaints.

  • Puncturing or cutting refrigeration components
  • Damaging pipes, electrical components, compressors, doors, hinges, shelves or seals through misuse or unreasonable handling
  • Using unauthorised tools or methods that alter the appliance
  • Allowing a third party to modify or repair the appliance without appropriate authorisation
  • Deliberate damage, concealment of relevant damage or materially false information concerning the condition of the appliance

Where SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD reasonably establishes that a repair is required because of customer-caused damage, the customer may be responsible for reasonable costs directly attributable to that damage, subject to applicable law and any required authorisation process.

15

Distinguishing Damage from Other Reported Faults

A refrigerator can have more than one issue. Where a customer reports a problem after physical damage has occurred, SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD may assess each reported issue separately. The assessment may consider:

  • Possible pre-existing defect
  • Customer-caused damage
  • Damage-related consequence
  • Repair-related issue
  • Normal operating condition
  • External or electrical condition
  • Wear associated with ordinary use
  • Issue requiring further diagnosis

The assessment may consider pre-delivery records, delivery photographs, complaint chronology, technician findings, repair records, product specifications and other relevant evidence. Neither a customer allegation nor a customer acknowledgement is, by itself, treated as conclusive proof of technical causation.

16

Repairs and Service Process

Where a refrigerator requires non-routine repair or service, SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD may use an internal or third-party technician. The customer may be asked to make the refrigerator available for inspection or collection at an agreed time. The process follows these steps:

  1. Record the reported problem.
  2. Collect reasonable supporting evidence.
  3. Assess or diagnose the reported condition.
  4. Identify whether the work appears to be warranty-related, customer-caused, non-warranty, or requires further investigation.
  5. Where required, provide an estimate and obtain the appropriate customer authorisation before chargeable repair work proceeds.
  6. Complete the agreed work and record the repair outcome where reasonably practical.
17

Repair Estimates and Authorisation

For chargeable repair or maintenance services to which applicable pre-authorisation rules apply, SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD will use an appropriate estimate and customer authorisation process. The estimate may identify the nature and extent of the work, parts or materials, labour, transport or call-out charges, applicable administration/service charges, validity period and any other material condition relevant to the service.

Where additional work becomes necessary after diagnosis, SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD may communicate the additional estimated cost and obtain the required authorisation before proceeding, except where applicable law permits another process.

SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD may publish or quote reasonable administrative, diagnostic, technician, transport, call-out or service charges for services that fall outside an applicable statutory warranty obligation or that are requested by the customer. Charges that may apply include:

  • Administrative handling of non-warranty service requests
  • Diagnostic attendance where a charge applies
  • Technician transport or call-out for non-warranty matters
  • Return visits not caused by an obligation of SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD

  • Additional delivery or collection services not included in the original order
No Unlawful Fee A charge will not be used as a device to avoid a statutory consumer remedy. Where applicable law requires SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD to provide a remedy at its own cost, that legal obligation takes priority.

20

Unauthorised Repairs and Third-Party Intervention

Customers should not arrange technical repairs, modifications, dismantling or component replacement through third parties where doing so may affect the appliance or the ability to determine the cause of a reported issue.

Where a third party has intervened, SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD may assess whether the intervention caused or contributed to the reported problem. Any effect on statutory rights will be determined in accordance with applicable law.

21

Normal Operating Sounds

Refrigeration equipment may make sounds associated with compressors, fans, thermostats, refrigerant movement, vibration and cycling. Examples can include humming, clicking, cycling or vibration.

A customer who believes a sound is abnormal should report it. SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD may request a recording or arrange an assessment. The fact that a unit is pre-owned does not, by itself, prove that every sound is normal or abnormal.

22

Electrical, Environmental and External Conditions

SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD may investigate whether an operational problem was caused or contributed to by circumstances outside the refrigerator, including power instability, electrical surges, unsuitable connections, improper installation, environmental conditions or customer modification. The actual circumstances and available evidence will be considered.

23

Returns and Change of Mind

SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD will honour any return, cancellation, replacement or refund right that applies under South African law.

  • A customer does not automatically obtain a general change-of-mind return right solely because they later prefer another model or dislike a disclosed cosmetic characteristic where no statutory right applies.
  • Any discretionary exchange or refund offered by SMART FREEZESA (PTY) LTD should be confirmed in writing and may have reasonable conditions.
  • Where an appliance must be returned or collected, the applicable return and collection process will be communicated to the customer.
  • Where a refund is due, the amount, method and timing will be handled in accordance with applicable law and the relevant transaction record.

  • Every replacement or exchange may be linked to the original invoice or order for record-keeping.
